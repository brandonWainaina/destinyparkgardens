A hotel built around water and green space.

Every guest room looks out over pool or garden. It's a layout we chose on purpose: the pools cool the terraces by day, the garden holds the light in the evening, and the two spaces hand off to each other as the day goes on.

  • Rooms and suites facing the pools or the garden lawn
  • On-site dining, terrace bar, and event catering
  • Dedicated event coordinator from enquiry to send-off
  • Parking and step-free access throughout the grounds
